Research Interests

My work develops artificial intelligence methods for fault detection and diagnosis in manufacturing systems, with an emphasis on problems where labeled data is insufficient and measured signals are noisy or highly uncertain.

Recent topics include unsupervised anomaly detection on vibration signals, mixed-type defect pattern recognition on wafer bin maps, explainable fault analysis, and prediction models for roll-to-roll battery electrode manufacturing.
